# Morning Story — 2026-11-17 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 25 · 감속)

## The Founder Problem

When the 71-year-old founder of a mid-sized Korean battery maker fell ill last spring, investors suddenly realized how much **key-person risk** they had been ignoring. There had been no **succession planning**; every major decision still crossed his desk. His **🔁playbook** had worked for three decades, but it left no one ready to take over. Morale was already slipping. Engineers who once stayed late were **quiet quitting**, doing exactly what their contracts required and nothing more, while several star researchers left for rivals in the U.S. — a **brain drain** the company could not afford. The interim CEO praised the **🔁versatility** of the remaining team, but analysts focused on harder numbers: **🔁like-for-like** sales fell 8% in the third quarter. "Talent, not technology, is now the constraint," one fund manager said.
