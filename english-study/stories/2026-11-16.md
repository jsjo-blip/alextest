# Morning Story — 2026-11-16 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 30 · 복습 집중)

## Closer to Home

For two decades, a Korean appliance maker kept almost no **safety stock**, trusting that parts from Asia would always arrive on time. Then a canal blockage and soaring freight rates left its Texas plant idle for three weeks. The CFO **🔁laid out** a blunt diagnosis for the board: the company could no longer **🔁operate on thin margins** and fragile supply lines at the same time. Rising shipping costs had already created a **🔁margin squeeze**, and customers were refusing further price hikes. The fix was **nearshoring**: moving key component suppliers from Asia to northern Mexico, just a day's drive from the plant. Labor would cost more, but the company would no longer need huge inventory buffers. Rather than retreat, management decided to **🔁double down on** the strategy, committing $400 million to a new supplier park in Monterrey.
