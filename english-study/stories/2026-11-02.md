## Good at Everything

At a 40-person food startup in Pangyo, the operations manager has to **wear many hats**: she negotiates with suppliers, runs the warehouse, and even answers customer emails on weekends. Her **🔁versatility** was a huge asset when the company was small. But this year, **🔁retaliatory tariffs** on imported spices caused a painful **🔁margin squeeze**, and investors began asking whether the firm needed specialists instead of a **jack of all trades**. With a **🔁credit crunch** making new funding hard to find, the CEO couldn't afford to hire three experts. Instead, he gave her a small team and asked her to focus on the work only she could do. "Being good at everything got us this far," he told the board. "Being great at a few things will get us further."
