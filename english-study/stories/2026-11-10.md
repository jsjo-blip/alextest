## Bold Pivot or Desperate Gamble

When rumours spread that property developer Saebom Construction might **🔁default on** its bonds, its shares suffered a brutal **🔁sell-off**, falling 40% in two days. Suppliers began demanding cash up front, creating a **🔁margin squeeze** just as steel prices rose. Saebom's other business, a home-internet subscription service, had its own problems: its **🔁churn rate** jumped after rivals cut prices. Worse, new **🔁retaliatory tariffs** on imported equipment made its planned data center far more expensive. Yet instead of retreating, the founder decided to **🔁double down on** the internet business, selling three office buildings to fund it. "Construction made us big," he told investors, "but subscriptions will keep us alive." Some called it a bold pivot; others called it a desperate gamble.
