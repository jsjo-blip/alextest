# Morning Story — 2026-11-13 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 27 · 복습 집중)

## When Everyone Owns the Same Stock

By early autumn, betting on chip stocks had become a **crowded trade**: hedge funds, pension funds, and retail investors all owned the same handful of names. Analysts praised the **🔁versatility** of one designer's chips, which powered everything from phones to data centers, and its board announced a $20 billion **🔁share buyback**. But when a cautious central bank speech dented investors' **🔁risk appetite**, the rush for the exit was brutal. Earnings forecasts barely changed; what fell was the price investors were willing to pay for them. This **multiple compression** cut the stock's price-to-earnings ratio from 45 to 30 in three weeks. "Selling the most popular stocks was the **🔁low-hanging fruit** for nervous funds," one strategist noted. "When a trade is this crowded, even good news can't stop the exodus."
