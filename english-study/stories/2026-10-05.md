# Morning Story — 2026-10-05 (Day 3)

## Reading the Governor

Every analyst in Seoul had been hoping for a **soft landing**, but when the central bank governor took the podium on Thursday, he **struck a hawkish tone**: inflation was still too high, he said, and the bank was "in no hurry" to cut rates. Within minutes the won strengthened, and bond traders began to map out the **knock-on effects** — dearer mortgages, slower consumer spending, and a tougher year for exporters already **facing stiff headwinds** in China. One economist warned that cutting rates too early could **stoke inflation** all over again, which was exactly what the governor seemed determined to avoid.

At a mid-sized auto-parts supplier in Ulsan, the CFO watched the press conference with her finance team. Their **burn rate** on a new EV components line was manageable, but the timing of a planned expansion had suddenly become the main **sticking point** with the board. Her advice was simple: **keep their powder dry**. The company had a modest **war chest** of retained earnings, and rather than spend it now on a factory that might sit half-idle, she wanted to wait until demand, and the rate outlook, became clearer. "When the cycle turns," she said, "the companies with cash will set the prices."

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **soft landing** | (경제가 침체 없이) 성장 둔화·물가 안정을 이루는 연착륙 | *Policymakers are trying to engineer a soft landing without triggering a recession.* |
| 2 | **strike a hawkish / dovish tone** | (통화정책에서) 매파적(긴축)/비둘기파적(완화) 기조를 보이다 | *The Fed chair struck a dovish tone, hinting at rate cuts next year.* |
| 3 | **knock-on effect** | 연쇄 효과, 파급 효과 | *The port strike had a knock-on effect on retailers across Europe.* |
| 4 | **stoke inflation** | 인플레이션을 부추기다 | *Critics argue that the new subsidies will only stoke inflation.* |
| 5 | **keep one's powder dry** | (기회를 위해) 자원·자금을 아껴두고 때를 기다리다 | *Many investors are keeping their powder dry until valuations come down.* |

## Review Expressions

- **face (stiff) headwinds** — 역풍/불리한 여건에 직면하다
- **war chest** — (인수·위기 대응용) 비축 자금
- **burn rate** — (스타트업의) 월간 현금 소진 속도
- **sticking point** — 협상의 걸림돌, 쟁점

## Bonus vocabulary (테스트 제외)

- **sit idle** — (설비·자금 등이) 놀고 있다, 가동되지 않다
