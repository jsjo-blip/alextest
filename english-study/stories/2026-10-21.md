# Morning Story — 2026-10-21 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 29 · 복습 집중)

## The 4,990-Won Chicken

Haneul Mart has a simple **loss leader**: a whole roast chicken sold at 4,990 won, below what it costs the company to make. The chicken loses money, but it drives **foot traffic**, and shoppers who come for dinner usually leave with wine, snacks and detergent. Last quarter, however, the strategy stopped working. Delivery apps pulled customers away from physical stores, and the chain **🔁missed estimates** by a wide margin. Its CEO admitted that small promotions no longer **🔁moved the needle**. Rivals sensed weakness, but Haneul still had a **🔁war chest** of nearly 2 trillion won. Rather than open new stores that could become **🔁stranded assets** if shopping keeps moving online, it decided to turn its existing branches into pickup hubs for online orders. "We'll still sell cheap chicken," the CEO said. "We just need people to come and get it."
