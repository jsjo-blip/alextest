## The Robot Bet

When Daon Electronics announced a $2 billion deal to buy a robotics startup, its shares fell 6%. Investors doubted the company could **🔁realize synergies** between home appliances and industrial robots, and many urged management to **🔁stick to its knitting**. The CEO disagreed. Appliance sales had stalled, she argued, and the company needed to **🔁pivot** before its core business shrank further. The startup's founders had **🔁driven a hard bargain**, securing a price nearly twice their last valuation, but Daon believed it could **🔁ramp up** robot production quickly using its own factories. Meanwhile, some hedge funds saw the sell-off as an overreaction and rushed to **🔁buy the dip**. "Markets hate uncertainty," one analyst wrote, "but the bigger risk would have been standing still while the industry moved on."
