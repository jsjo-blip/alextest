# Morning Story — 2026-11-04 (Lv.4 · 복습 대기 54 · 복습 전용)

## Everyone Had the Same Idea

Three years ago, battery maker Daejin Energy went on a spending spree, pouring $8 billion of **🔁capex** into new plants in Korea and the U.S. Management argued that the factories would help the company **🔁decouple** its supply chain from China. Today, the bet looks shakier. Too many rivals built plants at the same time, and the resulting **🔁overcapacity** has pushed battery prices down 25%. Cheaper imports are steadily **🔁eating into** Daejin's market share, and some carmakers now treat batteries almost as a **🔁loss leader**, selling them at cost to win long-term service contracts. Analysts estimate the **🔁payback period** for the U.S. plant at twelve years instead of six. "The strategy wasn't wrong," one board member said. "We just assumed nobody else would have the same idea."
