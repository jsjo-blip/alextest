# Morning Story — 2026-10-15 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 19 · 감속)

## Cutting Costs, Keeping Talent

After last month's **🔁sell-off** wiped 30% off its share price, Daon Electronics announced a **hiring freeze** across all non-engineering teams. Rather than cut jobs outright, the HR director planned to reduce costs through natural **attrition**, leaving roles unfilled as people retired or moved on. At the same time, the company would **upskill** factory supervisors in data analytics so they could take on work once done by outside consultants. Investors, caught up in a broad **🔁flight to safety**, were unimpressed at first. But one analyst argued the stock had probably **🔁bottomed out**. "Management is protecting cash without gutting the talent it will need when demand returns," she wrote. "That's exactly what you want to see in a downturn."
