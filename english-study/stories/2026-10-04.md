# Morning Story — 2026-10-04 (Day 2)

## The Term Sheet

Mina Park had eighteen months of runway left when she walked into the meeting with Northgate Ventures, but her **burn rate** was climbing faster than her revenue, and both sides knew it. Northgate opened with a valuation 20% below her last round — effectively a **down round** — arguing that the market had already **priced in** a long slowdown for logistics software. Mina had expected them to **drive a hard bargain**, and she was ready: over the summer she had **tightened the purse strings**, cut two unprofitable product lines, and could show that the company would break even within a year without a single new dollar.

The real **sticking point**, it turned out, wasn't the valuation at all but a clause that gave Northgate two seats on a five-person board. Mina refused to budge on control, yet she knew that walking away entirely might mean **leaving money on the table** at the worst possible moment. So she **hedged her bets**: she accepted a slightly lower price in exchange for a single board seat, and kept a smaller strategic investor in the room as a backup. When the term sheet arrived that evening, her co-founder read it twice and laughed. "We lost a little on paper," he said, "but we kept the company."

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **burn rate** | (스타트업의) 월간 현금 소진 속도 | *With a burn rate of $2 million a month, the startup needs to raise money by spring.* |
| 2 | **down round** | 이전 투자 라운드보다 낮은 기업가치로 받는 투자 | *The company avoided a down round by extending its existing investors' notes.* |
| 3 | **drive a hard bargain** | (협상에서) 강하게 밀어붙여 유리한 조건을 얻어내다 | *Their procurement team drives a hard bargain on every supplier contract.* |
| 4 | **sticking point** | (협상 타결을 막는) 걸림돌, 쟁점 | *Pricing was never the issue; the main sticking point was exclusivity.* |
| 5 | **leave money on the table** | (협상·거래에서) 더 얻을 수 있었던 이익을 놓치다 | *If you accept their first offer, you're probably leaving money on the table.* |

## Review Expressions

- **price in** — (시장이 사건·전망을) 가격에 이미 반영하다
- **tighten the purse strings** — 지출을 줄이다, 허리띠를 졸라매다
- **hedge one's bets** — 위험을 분산하다, 양다리를 걸치다
