# Morning Story — 2026-10-08 (Day 6)

## The Georgia Plant

Hanvolt, a Korean battery maker, had spent two years preparing to **ramp up** production at its new plant in Georgia. Demand was there — orders had surged **on the back of** U.S. tax credits for locally made EV parts — but the real **bottleneck** was skilled labor. Without enough trained technicians, half the production lines sat idle, and the company was **facing stiff headwinds** from cheaper Chinese rivals in every other market. The plant was part of a broader wave of **reshoring**, as American automakers pushed suppliers to build closer to home.

At the quarterly review in Seoul, the sales director proposed **passing on** higher wage costs **to** customers through a 4% price increase. The CFO hesitated: their biggest client, a Detroit automaker, had already made price the main **sticking point** in contract talks. Meanwhile, the U.S. Federal Reserve had just **struck a hawkish tone**, which meant financing for a second plant would be expensive. In the end the board agreed to **keep its powder dry** on expansion, focus on training, and revisit the second plant in six months.

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **ramp up** (production / hiring) | (생산·채용 등을) 단계적으로 늘리다, 증산하다 | *Samsung plans to ramp up production of its new chips next quarter.* |
| 2 | **on the back of** (sth) | ~에 힘입어, ~ 덕분에 | *Exports rose 12% on the back of strong semiconductor demand.* |
| 3 | **bottleneck** | (생산·공정의) 병목, 걸림돌이 되는 지점 | *The shortage of transformers has become a major bottleneck for data centers.* |
| 4 | **reshoring** (n.) / **reshore** (v.) | (해외 생산 시설을) 자국으로 되돌리기, 리쇼어링 | *Reshoring has accelerated since the pandemic exposed supply chain risks.* |
| 5 | **pass on** (costs) **to** (sb) | (비용 상승분을) ~에게 전가하다 | *Airlines passed on higher fuel costs to passengers through surcharges.* |

## Review Expressions

- **face (stiff) headwinds** — 역풍/불리한 여건에 직면하다
- **sticking point** — 협상의 걸림돌, 쟁점
- **strike a hawkish / dovish tone** — 매파적/비둘기파적 기조를 보이다
- **keep one's powder dry** — 자금을 아껴두고 때를 기다리다
