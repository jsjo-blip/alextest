## Growing Without Losing Trust

Facing slower demand for its frozen dumplings, food giant Hanseong decided to **🔁scale back** its expansion in Europe and **🔁cut its guidance** for the year. The company had long relied on **🔁economies of scale**: a giant factory in Incheon that produced dumplings more cheaply than any rival. But that advantage weakened as energy and flour prices soared. Management briefly considered **🔁shrinkflation**, quietly cutting each bag from 1 kg to 900 g, but the marketing team warned that customers would notice within weeks. Instead, Hanseong froze new **🔁capex** and focused on automating its existing lines. Economists, meanwhile, warned that food companies raising prices at the same time could **🔁stoke inflation** just as the central bank hoped to cut rates. "Our job isn't to grow at any cost," the CFO said. "It's to grow without losing our customers' trust."
