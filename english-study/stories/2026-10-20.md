# Morning Story — 2026-10-20 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 25 · 감속)

## Saving the System

When property developer Taeyang Construction **defaulted on** a $300 million bond payment last spring, regional banks suddenly stopped lending to anyone in the sector. The resulting **credit crunch** forced builders across the country to **🔁scale back** projects, and several listed developers **🔁cut their guidance** for the year. Smaller lenders, heavily exposed to unfinished apartment complexes, begged the government for help, but officials refused to **bail out** shareholders who had ignored the warning signs. Instead, they set up a fund that bought half-built projects at steep discounts. By autumn, analysts said housing starts had finally **🔁bottomed out**, though few expected a quick rebound. "The state saved the system, not the speculators," one central bank official said. "That distinction matters if we want people to take risk seriously next time."
