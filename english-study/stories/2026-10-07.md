# Morning Story — 2026-10-07 (Day 5)

## The Strategy Offsite

The leadership team of Ridewell, a car-maintenance platform, spent two days at a hotel outside Seoul trying to answer one question: where should next year's money go? The head of growth argued for expanding into insurance and used-car sales, but the CEO was skeptical. Their last attempt to diversify had ended in a **down round**, and he wanted the company to **stick to its knitting**. "We've already picked the **low-hanging fruit** in maintenance," the COO admitted, "but we still don't have the **economies of scale** to compete on price with the big dealer networks."

By the second afternoon, the debate had narrowed. Small tweaks to the app, the CFO pointed out, simply wouldn't **move the needle** on revenue; the only initiative large enough to matter was a fleet-maintenance service for logistics companies. Instead of **hedging their bets** across five projects, the team agreed to **double down on** fleets. The CFO added one caution: if the economy failed to achieve a **soft landing**, the **knock-on effects** on freight volumes would hit their new customers first. "Then we'll be the cheapest option they have," the CEO replied, "and that's exactly when they'll call us."

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **stick to one's knitting** | (다각화하지 않고) 잘하는 본업에 집중하다 | *After the failed acquisition, the board told management to stick to its knitting.* |
| 2 | **low-hanging fruit** | 쉽게 얻을 수 있는 성과, 손쉬운 목표 | *Cutting duplicate software licenses was the low-hanging fruit in our cost review.* |
| 3 | **economies of scale** | 규모의 경제 | *Larger battery makers enjoy economies of scale that startups can't match.* |
| 4 | **move the needle** | (가시적인) 의미 있는 변화를 만들다 | *A 1% discount won't move the needle; we need a completely new offer.* |
| 5 | **double down on** (sth) | (위험을 감수하고) ~에 더 집중·투자하다 | *Despite the slowdown, the company is doubling down on AI research.* |

## Review Expressions

- **down round** — 이전 라운드보다 낮은 기업가치의 투자
- **hedge one's bets** — 위험을 분산하다, 양다리를 걸치다
- **soft landing** — 경제 연착륙
- **knock-on effect** — 연쇄 효과, 파급 효과
