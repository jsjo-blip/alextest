# Morning Story — 2026-10-13 (Lv.3)

## Red Tuesday

Markets opened Tuesday in a sharp **sell-off** after a weak jobs report from Washington, and by noon traders were describing a classic **flight to safety**: gold and U.S. Treasuries rallied while the KOSPI slid 3%. Chip stocks, which had soared **🔁on the back of** AI demand all year, fell hardest. At a Yeouido brokerage, junior analyst Jiwoo Han watched her clients' **risk appetite** vanish within hours. Her manager was calmer. Exporters were already **🔁facing stiff headwinds**, he admitted, but memory makers were still **🔁ramping up** capacity, and orders hadn't slowed. "Prices usually **bottom out** before the news improves," he said. "Disciplined investors will **buy the dip** next week, while everyone else is still reading the headlines."

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **sell-off** | (주식 등의) 대량 매도, 급락 | *Tech shares led a broad sell-off after the central bank's decision.* |
| 2 | **flight to safety** | 안전자산 선호 (위험자산 → 안전자산 자금 이동) | *The banking scare triggered a flight to safety into gold and government bonds.* |
| 3 | **risk appetite** | 위험 선호도, 위험을 감수하려는 의향 | *Investors' risk appetite has returned as inflation cools.* |
| 4 | **bottom out** | (가격·경기가) 바닥을 치다 | *Analysts expect housing prices to bottom out early next year.* |
| 5 | **buy the dip** | 하락장에서 저가 매수하다 | *Retail investors rushed to buy the dip after the 10% drop.* |
