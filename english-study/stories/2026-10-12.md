# Morning Story — 2026-10-12 (Lv.3)

## The Grocery Bid

When Hanseo Retail launched a **hostile takeover** bid for Mapleleaf Mart, the smaller grocer's board scrambled to find a **white knight**. Within weeks, a logistics group stepped in with a friendlier offer, arguing that combining the two delivery networks would end a costly **🔁price war** and help them **realize synergies** of nearly $90 million a year. But **due diligence** turned up an awkward detail: Mapleleaf's new discount app was already **🔁cannibalizing** sales at its own stores. The buyer could live with that; the real **deal-breaker** was a pending lawsuit over unpaid overtime. After two tense weeks, the lawyers agreed to cap the liability, and the logistics group announced plans to **🔁ramp up** same-day delivery across Seoul by spring.
