# Morning Story — 2026-10-06 (Day 4)

## Behind the Headline Number

On paper, Corvane Retail had a great quarter: revenue **beat analysts' estimates** by 6%, and the stock jumped in after-hours trading. But Daniel Seo, a veteran equity analyst, had learned to read past the headline. Buried in the footnotes was a **write-down** of $180 million on unsold winter inventory, and once he stripped out a one-off gain from a property sale, the company's **bottom line** had actually shrunk. Nobody was accusing management of **cooking the books** — every number was in the filing — but the presentation seemed designed to **paper over the cracks** in its core business.

On the earnings call, Daniel pressed the CFO on margins, and she pushed back firmly. Analysts joked afterwards that she **drove a hard bargain** even with questions. Still, she admitted the company had begun to **tighten the purse strings**, freezing new store openings for a year. By the next morning, the share price had given back all of its gains. "The market had **priced in** a weak holiday season anyway," Daniel wrote in his note to clients. "Investors who sold into yesterday's rally probably didn't **leave much money on the table**."

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **beat (/ miss) estimates** | (실적이) 시장 예상치를 웃돌다(/밑돌다) | *Samsung's operating profit beat estimates thanks to strong memory chip prices.* |
| 2 | **write-down** (n.) / **write down** (v.) | (자산 가치의) 감액, 상각 | *The bank took a $2 billion write-down on its commercial real estate loans.* |
| 3 | **bottom line** | 순이익, 최종 결과; (비유) 핵심 | *The new pricing strategy added $30 million to the bottom line.* |
| 4 | **cook the books** | 장부를 조작하다, 분식회계를 하다 | *The executives were jailed for cooking the books for nearly a decade.* |
| 5 | **paper over the cracks** | (근본 문제를 해결하지 않고) 문제를 임시로 덮다 | *Another round of discounts will only paper over the cracks in the brand's appeal.* |

## Review Expressions

- **drive a hard bargain** — 협상에서 강하게 밀어붙이다
- **tighten the purse strings** — 지출을 줄이다, 허리띠를 졸라매다
- **price in** — (시장이 사건·전망을) 가격에 이미 반영하다
- **leave money on the table** — 더 얻을 수 있었던 이익을 놓치다
