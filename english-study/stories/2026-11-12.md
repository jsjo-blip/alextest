# Morning Story — 2026-11-12 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 12 · 정상)

## The Price of Being Noticed

For years, the outdoor-gear brand Pine & Peak relied on **word of mouth**: hikers recommended its jackets to friends, and **🔁foot traffic** at its flagship stores did the rest. But as rivals flooded social media with ads, its **customer acquisition cost** nearly doubled, and discount campaigns barely **🔁moved the needle**. The new CMO took a different route. Instead of cutting prices, she bet on **premiumization**, launching a $600 expedition line that few customers would buy but many would notice. The gamble produced a **halo effect**: sales of its ordinary fleeces jumped as the brand suddenly looked more serious. Store staff were trained to **upsell** shoppers from basic jackets to waterproof models, and the company **🔁front-loaded** its winter marketing into October. Analysts now say Pine & Peak has rebuilt its **brand equity** without starting a price war.
