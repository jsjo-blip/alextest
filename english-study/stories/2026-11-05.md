## Better Be Different

When the streaming service Ondi raised its monthly fee by 30%, investors hoped it had pricing power. Instead, its **🔁churn rate** nearly doubled, and the shares suffered a sharp **🔁sell-off**. Critics also accused the company of **🔁shrinkflation**: the higher price came with fewer original shows and more ads. Management argued that rising content costs, not greed, were the problem, and that bigger rivals enjoyed **🔁economies of scale** Ondi could only dream of. Economists, meanwhile, warned that subscription price hikes across the industry could **🔁stoke inflation** in the services sector. Rather than retreat, the CEO decided to **🔁double down on** Korean dramas, signing a $500 million deal with a major studio. "We can't be cheaper than Netflix," she told staff. "So we'd better be different."
