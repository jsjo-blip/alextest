# Morning Story — 2026-10-14 (Lv.3)

## Finding the Right Users

Eighteen months after launch, Parcelo, a Seoul-based logistics app, still hadn't found **product-market fit**. Downloads looked fine, but the **churn rate** was brutal: six out of ten users disappeared within a month. Small fixes to the sign-up flow never **🔁moved the needle**, so the founders made a bold call to **pivot** from consumers to small online sellers. They **scaled back** their TV ads, rewrote the **go-to-market strategy** around direct sales, and **🔁doubled down on** the one feature merchants loved: same-day pickup. By the next quarter, revenue had **🔁beaten estimates** by 30%, and churn had fallen below 10%. "We didn't need more users," the CEO told investors. "We needed the right ones."
