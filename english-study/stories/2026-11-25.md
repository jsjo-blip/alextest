# Morning Story — 2026-11-25 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 68 · 복습 전용)

## Back to Basics

After a decade of acquisitions, a Korean food conglomerate had built a $3 billion **🔁war chest** and a habit of spending it. Its **🔁playbook** never changed: buy a fast-growing brand, promise to **🔁realize synergies**, and move on to the next deal. But most of those savings never appeared, and the newest units were losing money. When a new CEO arrived, she decided to **🔁pivot**. The company would **🔁stick to its knitting** — sauces and noodles — and sell three businesses it barely understood. Some board members resisted; **🔁loss aversion** made them reluctant to sell assets for less than they had paid. "Admitting a mistake is painful," the CEO said, "but holding on to it is far more expensive."
