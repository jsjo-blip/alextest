# Morning Story — 2026-10-16 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 21 · 감속)

## Cash Tells the Truth

Hallim Foods surprised investors on Friday by **cutting its full-year guidance** for the second time in three months. The culprit was a classic **margin squeeze**: wheat and packaging costs kept climbing, but fierce competition from discount chains made it impossible to raise prices. Sales were still growing **🔁on the back of** strong exports to Southeast Asia, and the company had just **🔁ramped up** production at its new Vietnam factory. Yet **free cash flow** had turned negative for the first time in a decade. The CFO promised to **🔁tighten the purse strings**, delaying two marketing campaigns and renegotiating supplier contracts. "Revenue tells you where you're going," she told analysts, "but cash tells you whether you'll get there."
