## Turbulence Ahead

Budget airlines **🔁operate on thin margins**, so small changes matter. When two low-cost carriers started a **🔁price war** on routes to Japan last summer, fares fell 30% and rising fuel bills began to **🔁eat into** profits. Yet Korea Sky's third-quarter results looked surprisingly strong. A closer look showed why: a gain from selling three old aircraft had **🔁flattered** earnings, while a $60 million **🔁one-off charge** for engine repairs was tucked away as an "adjusted" item. Analysts warned that **🔁operating leverage** cuts both ways. With most costs fixed, a 5% drop in passengers could wipe out a quarter of operating profit. "The headline number is fine," one analyst said, "but the business underneath is flying straight into headwinds."
