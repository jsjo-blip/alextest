# Morning Story — 2026-10-26 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 23 · 감속)

## Caught in the Middle

Korean exporters **🔁faced stiff headwinds** this quarter as Washington threatened new tariffs on electronics. Expecting the duties to take effect in January, many U.S. buyers rushed to **front-load** their orders, and shipments surged in September. Analysts warned that the boom was simply borrowed from the future. Beijing, meanwhile, announced **retaliatory tariffs** on American farm goods, and both sides talked openly about **decoupling** their tech supply chains. Caught in the middle, Seoul-based component maker Hanil Tech **🔁cut its guidance** for next year and decided to **🔁scale back** a planned factory in Texas until the rules became clearer. "We can survive high tariffs," its CFO said. "What we can't survive is not knowing what they'll be next month."
