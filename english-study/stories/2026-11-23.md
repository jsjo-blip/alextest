## Bad News, Delivered Early

Before Monday's budget meeting, the finance director sent the CEO a short email: "I wanted to **🔁flag** a problem before it surprises the board." Rising labor and freight costs had created a **🔁margin squeeze** in the company's largest division, and **🔁free cash flow** would fall well short of forecasts. Her proposal was simple: a **🔁hiring freeze** for non-essential roles until spring, and no new acquisitions. Some executives wanted to **🔁double down on** the expansion plan instead, arguing that cutting back now would signal weakness. But after two hours of walking through the numbers, the CEO said he felt more **🔁grounded** about the choices ahead. "I'd rather hear bad news early," he told the team, "than read about it in the quarterly report."
