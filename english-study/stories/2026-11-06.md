## Buying the People

When a Korean chip-equipment maker announced a plan to buy a smaller German rival, investors were divided. The buyer had a **🔁war chest** of nearly 3 trillion won, and the deal promised to remove a key **🔁bottleneck** in its supply of precision lenses. But talks dragged on for months. The main **🔁sticking point** was whether the German founders could keep running their research lab. Meanwhile, the chip cycle seemed to have **🔁bottomed out**, and some board members argued the company should **🔁pivot** to buying back shares instead. Analysts also warned of a **🔁knock-on effect**: if the deal collapsed, two other suppliers might raise their prices. In the end, the buyer agreed to let the founders stay for three years. "We weren't buying machines," its CEO said. "We were buying the people who know how to build them."
