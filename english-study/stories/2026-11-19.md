## The Long Goodbye

Few chief executives manage their exit well. When the founder of a Seoul-based cosmetics group finally stepped down last year, the company had done no real **🔁succession planning**, and three senior executives left within months — a **🔁brain drain** that rattled investors. The new CEO moved quickly to **🔁position the company as** a premium skincare brand rather than a mass-market player. But the numbers were sobering: **🔁like-for-like** sales in its home market fell for a fourth straight quarter, and **🔁multiple compression** cut its valuation from 30 times earnings to 18. Analysts say the urge to cling to familiar strategies is **🔁hard-wired** in founder-led companies. "The hardest part of a handover," one board member admitted, "is accepting that what worked yesterday won't work tomorrow."
