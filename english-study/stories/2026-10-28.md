# Morning Story — 2026-10-28 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 26 · 복습 집중)

## Five Grams Lighter

When **🔁retaliatory tariffs** raised the cost of imported cocoa and packaging, snack maker Lotus Foods faced a choice: raise prices or quietly shrink its products. It chose **shrinkflation**, cutting its chocolate bars from 50 to 45 grams while keeping the price the same. Shoppers noticed within weeks. Social media filled with angry posts, **🔁foot traffic** at its flagship stores fell, and the company **🔁missed estimates** for the quarter. Its rival Haetbit, by contrast, had real **pricing power**: loyal customers accepted a 10% price rise without complaint. Lotus struggled inside, too. Staff **🔁attrition** reached 18% as frustrated sales teams left for competitors. "You can hide a price increase for a month," one analyst said, "but not from people who buy your product every day."
