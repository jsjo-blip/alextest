## Panic Creates Bargains

When rumours spread that a major Chinese property developer was close to collapse, Asian markets suffered their worst **🔁sell-off** in two years. A classic **🔁flight to safety** followed: investors dumped stocks and piled into U.S. Treasuries and gold. Banks, fearing another **🔁credit crunch**, began to **🔁tighten the purse strings**, cutting credit lines even to healthy borrowers. Seoul-based Mirae Shipping was forced to **🔁scale back** its plan to buy six new vessels. Its share price fell so far that an activist fund launched a **🔁hostile takeover** bid, arguing that the company was worth more broken up than as a whole. "Panic creates bargains," the fund's manager said, "but only for those who still have cash."
