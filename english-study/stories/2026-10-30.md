# Morning Story — 2026-10-30 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 22 · 감속)

## A Bet Nobody Can See

Last year, chipmaker Hanul Semi announced a record $20 billion in **capex** for three new factories, betting that demand for AI chips would keep growing. This year, investors are less excited. Rivals in Taiwan and China built plants too, and analysts now warn of global **overcapacity** that could push prices down by 30%. Hanul is already feeling a **🔁margin squeeze** as costs rise faster than prices. Shareholders want the company to cancel one factory and spend the money on a **🔁share buyback** instead. Management resisted at first, but it has started picking the **🔁low-hanging fruit**: cutting consultants and delaying office upgrades. The real question is the **payback period**. "A factory that takes eight years to pay for itself," one investor said, "is a bet on a world nobody can see."
