# Morning Story — 2026-10-27 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 17 · 감속)

## Someone Has to Run It

Shares of Daehan Retail had gone nowhere for five years, so few were surprised when an **activist investor** bought a 7% stake and demanded change. The fund argued that management was sitting on too much cash: the company generated strong **🔁free cash flow** but spent little of it. Its first demand was a $500 million **share buyback**, which it called **🔁low-hanging fruit** that would lift the stock price immediately. The board refused, warning that a **🔁credit crunch** could arrive at any time and the cash might be needed. The activist responded by launching a **proxy fight**, asking other shareholders to vote in three new directors at the March meeting. "We're not trying to run the company," its founder said. "We're trying to make sure someone does."
