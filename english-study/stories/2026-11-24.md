## When the Story Changes

For two years, Mexican industrial real estate was a **🔁crowded trade**: every fund wanted exposure to **🔁nearshoring**, as manufacturers moved factories closer to the U.S. market. Then the story changed. Tariff threats from Washington made investors question whether the boom would last, and **🔁multiple compression** followed quickly. One warehouse developer's shares fell 25% even though its rental income was still growing. Its **🔁like-for-like** rents — excluding newly built properties — rose 9% last quarter, yet the market barely noticed. "The urge to sell when everyone else is selling seems **🔁hard-wired**," one portfolio manager said. "But the fundamentals haven't changed nearly as much as the prices have."
