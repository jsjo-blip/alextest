# Morning Story — 2026-10-29 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 39 · 복습 전용)

## Every Point Counts

Two years ago, Mirae Coffee used a cheap Americano as a **🔁loss leader** to fill its cafés. Then bean prices doubled, and the chain began to **🔁face stiff headwinds**. Its new premium cold-brew line also started to **🔁cannibalize** sales of its regular drinks instead of attracting new customers. In July, management **🔁cut its guidance** for the year and decided to **🔁scale back** its plan to open 200 new stores. The biggest test came when its main supplier, a Brazilian cooperative, demanded a 30% price increase. Mirae's purchasing chief **🔁drove a hard bargain**, threatening to switch to Vietnamese beans, and the two sides finally settled on 12%. "In a bad year," she said, "every percentage point is a small victory."
