# Morning Story — 2026-11-03 (Lv.4 · 복습 대기 54 · 복습 전용)

## Come for the Experience

Seoul's oldest department store has been **🔁facing stiff headwinds** for years. Online shopping drained **🔁foot traffic** from its flagship branch, and last quarter management **🔁cut its guidance** for the third time. Small fixes, like longer opening hours, failed to **🔁move the needle**. So the new CEO took a bolder approach. She decided to **🔁scale back** the floor space devoted to clothing by half and rent it to restaurants, galleries and a climbing gym. When a popular bakery chain demanded a rent-free first year, she **🔁drove a hard bargain**, offering only three months free in exchange for a share of its sales. Six months later, weekend visits are up 40%. "People don't come to buy shirts anymore," she said. "They come for an experience, and then they buy shirts."
