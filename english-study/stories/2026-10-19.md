# Morning Story — 2026-10-19 (Lv.3 · 복습 대기 26 · 복습 집중)

## Green on Paper

Hanbit Energy **🔁beat estimates** for the third quarter in a row, yet its share price fell 8% on Monday after an environmental group accused it of **greenwashing**: the company had been advertising itself as "carbon-neutral by 2040" while quietly approving two new coal plants. The **🔁knock-on effect** was swift. A Dutch pension fund put its stake under review, and lenders began asking whether the coal plants would become **stranded assets** once carbon taxes rose. Under pressure, the CEO announced a plan to **🔁pivot** toward offshore wind, but turbine makers, knowing Hanbit had few alternatives, **🔁drove a hard bargain** on price. "We can't advertise our way out of this," one director admitted. "We have to change what we actually build."
