# Morning Story — 2026-10-09 (Day 7)

## The Instant Noodle War

For thirty years, Daeil Foods' classic spicy ramen had been the company's **cash cow**, quietly funding everything from new factories to overseas marketing. Then two rivals launched premium "restaurant-style" noodles at aggressive prices, and within a year they had **eaten into** Daeil's market share in convenience stores. The board panicked and cut prices by 15%, triggering a **price war** that hurt everyone's **bottom line**. Discounts, as one director admitted, only **papered over the cracks**: the real problem was that younger shoppers saw the brand as old-fashioned.

The new marketing head proposed launching a low-sodium premium line, but the CFO worried it would **cannibalize** sales of the original product rather than win new customers. They tested it quietly in Busan first. To everyone's surprise, the line **gained traction** with office workers in their twenties, and most of its buyers had never purchased Daeil before. Still, after last year's $40 million **write-down** on a failed snack brand, the board wanted proof before a national rollout. And with food prices already high, nobody wanted to be accused of helping **stoke inflation** with another price increase.

## Today's New Expressions

| # | Expression | 뜻 | Example |
|---|-----------|----|---------|
| 1 | **cash cow** | (안정적으로 큰 수익을 내는) 효자 상품·사업, 캐시카우 | *Search advertising remains Google's biggest cash cow.* |
| 2 | **eat into** (sth) | (이익·점유율·예산 등을) 잠식하다, 갉아먹다 | *Rising labor costs are eating into restaurant margins.* |
| 3 | **price war** | 가격 전쟁, 출혈 경쟁 | *The delivery apps' price war left none of them profitable.* |
| 4 | **cannibalize** (sales) | (자사 신제품이 기존 제품의) 매출을 잠식하다 | *Apple accepted that the iPhone would cannibalize iPod sales.* |
| 5 | **gain traction** | (제품·아이디어가) 호응을 얻다, 자리를 잡아가다 | *The subscription plan is finally gaining traction with small businesses.* |
