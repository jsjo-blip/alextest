# Morning Story — 2026-11-11 (Lv.5 · 복습 대기 18 · 감속)

## Reading Between the Lines

On paper, Daejin Electronics had a decent quarter. But analysts who read the footnotes were less impressed. Profit had been **flattered** by the sale of an old warehouse, while a $90 million **one-off charge** for a product recall was quietly excluded from "adjusted" earnings. Strip out both, and **like-for-like** sales in its home-appliance unit fell 4%, the third straight decline. Worse, the company's heavy fixed costs meant that **operating leverage** was working in reverse: every small dip in revenue hit margins disproportionately. **🔁Retaliatory tariffs** on its washing machines in the U.S. added to the pain. Management responded by **🔁cutting its guidance** for the full year and promising to **🔁scale back** production at two overseas plants. "The headline numbers look fine," one analyst wrote, "but the underlying business is shrinking."

## Today's New Expressions (주제: 회계·실적 분석)

- **flatter (results / figures)** — (실적·수치를) 실제보다 좋아 보이게 하다
- **one-off charge** — 일회성 비용(손실)
- **like-for-like (sales)** — 동일 기준의; 기존점 기준 매출
- **operating leverage** — 영업 레버리지 (매출 변화가 이익에 증폭되는 효과)

## Review Expressions

- **retaliatory tariffs** — 보복 관세
- **cut its guidance** — 실적 전망치를 하향하다
- **scale back** — (규모·계획을) 축소하다
