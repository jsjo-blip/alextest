## C. 상황에 가장 알맞은 표현 고르기

7. A struggling cafe chain repaints all its stores and launches a celebrity ad campaign, but its coffee is still mediocre and its rents are still too high. The makeover only ________.
   - (a) beats estimates
   - (b) papers over the cracks
   - (c) cooks the books
   - (d) prices it in
8. A startup last valued at $500 million raises new money at a $300 million valuation. This is called a ________.
   - (a) write-down
   - (b) soft landing
   - (c) down round
   - (d) war chest
