## A. 빈칸 채우기 (알맞은 형태로)

2. Rather than accept a ________ ________ that would dilute early employees, the founders chose to cut costs and wait.
3. The landlord ________ a ________ ________: a ten-year lease, no rent-free period, and a 5% annual increase.
4. Korean exporters are ________ ________ ________ from a strong won and weaker demand in China.

## C. 상황에 가장 알맞은 표현 고르기

7. Both companies agreed on price and delivery dates, but the deal stalled because neither would accept liability for shipping delays. The liability clause was the ________.
   - (a) war chest  (b) sticking point  (c) burn rate  (d) down round
8. The central bank cut rates as expected, yet stock prices didn't move at all. Why?
   - (a) Investors had already priced it in.
   - (b) Investors were driving a hard bargain.
   - (c) Investors were leaving money on the table.
   - (d) Investors were facing headwinds.
